## 2. Kai's format (decided Nov 18: cinematic + bold)

- Kai talks full screen, one continuous take (HeyGen, the "Zoom olive tee" look, with emotion tags).
- Cut to AI-generated shots of Kai acting out each line every 2–3 seconds (Higgsfield, about 1.5 credits a still,
  35 credits for 5 seconds of motion).
- Giant yellow (#FFE14D) headline words, bold condensed font, whoosh and boom sounds. No music baked in.
- **Cover template:** Kai's face + the tool name in giant yellow + a 2–3 word result. The same template on every post.

## 3. Content mix (2 reels a day, posted to both Instagram and TikTok)

The 10% app share follows Kai's rule of four pure-value videos for every one featuring your apps, and those
videos say plainly that they're your apps (#ad or "made by the team").

## 4. Distribution for your apps

1. **The bio link is your own app.** Put Kai's link-in-bio on bio.bar (dogfooding is a demo in itself),
   with one tracked link per app (`?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=kai&utm_campaign=<reel>`), the same way @seanaiux does.
2. **Comment-keyword → DM → app.** Ootto auto-reply sends the file. The DM also carries the app link.
   Turn on the Ootto follow-gate so the file only arrives once they follow, which is the most direct way to grow followers.
3. **Built with Kai** demos (series 5) show the app solving a real problem. They never open with "download my app".
4. Every app demo gets its own UTM, so we can see which reel actually sends installs.
